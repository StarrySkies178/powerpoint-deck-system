# House style rules

Source: the shared consulting rules in `Style/style-findings.md`. Values marked *candidate* are proposals waiting on the decisions below.

## Decisions

| Decision | Status |
|---|---|
| Slide format | **Decided: 16:9** (DECK-SYSTEM.md defaults). References are 4:3: re-proportion, never stretch |
| Colour identity (main colour, accent, greys, hex codes) | **Open**: pick a base deck or set your own palette |

## Canvas and grid (candidate)
- 16:9, 13.33 × 7.5 in. Margins and column grid: TBD with the master template.
- Boxes in a row are the same size, with equal gaps between them.

## Slide anatomy (same position on every slide)
- Title top-left, an action title of at most 2 lines, with a thin rule or clear space below it.
- Optional unit or subtitle line under the title (e.g. "US$ m, 2020–24").
- One body area.
- Optional takeaway box at the bottom for the "so what".
- Footer: source and footnotes bottom-left, page number bottom-right.

## Type (candidate)
- One sans-serif family.
- Sizes: title 20–30 pt (pick one), subtitle 14–18 pt bold, body 12–16 pt, chart labels 10–11 pt, footnotes 8–9 pt. Exact scale TBD.

## Colour (roles fixed, values open)
- **Main colour** plus a **set of greys** do most of the work. Grey gives context, and colour marks the point.
- **One accent**, used only on the key point.
- **Dark neutral** for start and end bars, text and rules.
- Hex codes: TBD.

## Charts
- Data labels on; few or no gridlines.
- Grey for "others"; the main colour for the series that matters.
- Flat 2D only. No 3D, shadows or gradients.
