## 1. Reading the brief, then answering it

New in this batch: each case came with its brief, so the decks can be checked against the question.

- The briefs are one page: two to four context bullets with bold key phrases, then 4–5 questions ("market and channels, competition, customer, store expansion potential, other growth"; "market drivers, competition, target consumers and KPCs, channel performance"; "market status, pain points, where to start, which services, strategic actions"). The 2022 final round adds "2–3 focus dimensions, adjacency, revenue forecast with assumptions, risks and mitigants".
- **The decks follow the brief's question list closely**: the analysis sections come in roughly the brief's order (market, then customer and competition, then the client's position), each question gets its own slide or section, and the executive summary's situation boxes match the brief's topics. Where the brief asks for a forecast with assumptions, the decks show the assumptions on the slide (formula lines, assumption tables), not only in the appendix.
- The 2024 brief asks about social value as well as profit, and three of four 2024 decks answer it with a dedicated figure: a balance scale of profit vs responsibility (BASIL 12), a "creating shared value" philosophy panel (CVCJ 14), a social-responsibility column (BSTORM 11, YESS 10).

## 2. The story skeleton

The same spine as the earlier studies (`case-competitions.md` §1, `case-competitions-2019-2020.md` §1), tighter:

1. Cover → executive summary (always slide 2 or 3) → agenda tracker (10 of 13 decks; 23 tracker slides in all)
2. Market → customer → competition (each one slide, never more than two)
3. Diagnosis of the client (pain points, value-chain weaknesses)
4. **Strategy overview with a named strategy** (all 13 decks; see §3)
5. One slide per pillar, usually two for the biggest pillar
6. Financials: revenue bridge by strategy (10 waterfalls in 9 decks) + scenarios
7. **Implementation on one slide**: gantt + forecast + risk map together (7 decks: ChillChili 14, Fusion 14, IIVA 17, Shibaco 14, BSTORM 15, CVCJ 13, YESS 14), or gantt and risk map on separate slides (4 decks)
8. Appendix: unit economics, P&L models, city lists, questionnaires

Body length is short: 13–19 slides before the appendix, 11–15 content slides (executive summary included). Nothing in the body is a pure text slide.

## 3. The named strategy and its letters

Every deck names its strategy, and most build the name from letters or numbers that come back on every pillar slide:

| Deck | Name | How it is shown |
|---|---|---|
| ChillChili | "2+1" operating model, "3S" supply-chain strategy | big "3S" with red banner heads joined by plus signs |
| IIVA | "3E" strategy | big italic red letters heading three strategy boxes |
| Shibaco | "deeper, closer, stronger" | lettered A/B/C column of dark photo tiles; the word is the slide's tag |
| Fusion | "Pioneering War" and "Defensive War" | two named wars, lettered levers A1, A2, B1, B2 reused as title tags |
| WXYZ, YESS | "3+1" | three product or brand moves plus one enabler |
| CVCJ | "4 sights" (Insight, Oversight, Hindsight, Foresight) and "SPV" | large-initial red banners; each pillar title starts with its sight word |
| BSTORM | "POP" (Place, Operation, Promotion) | large initials on dark photo pillars; each pillar title starts with its letter word |
| WhyConsulting | location / format / assortment / operating model | a tab bar above every pillar slide with the active tab red |

**New devices:** the strategy **tab bar** (WhyConsulting 10–13: four grey tabs above the title, the current one red) and the **acronym banner** (a large initial letter in a colour block with the rest of the word beside it, CVCJ, BSTORM). Both do what the breadcrumb does in the 2019–20 decks, more visibly.

## 4. How they write the executive summary

The forms of the earlier studies return (situation → pillars → outcome is now the default). Measured on the 13 summaries:

| Form | How it looks | Seen in |
|---|---|---|
| **Situation → strategy → result (T1B2)** | three outlined situation boxes (market / customer / company) on top, a coloured strategy bar, 3–4 lettered pillar boxes, then a result bar with the target number | ChillChili, Starlight, TrueNorth, BeAnInnovator, Fusion, WXYZ, BASIL, CVCJ, YESS (9 of 13) |
| **Stage rows (ROWS)** | a column of stage labels down the left (situation / problem / goal / strategy / impact) joined by a down arrow, each row filled across | WhyConsulting, IIVA, BSTORM |
| **Four numbered columns (COL4)** | numbered red heads, outlined sub-boxes, red triangle, then lettered strategy tiles with small forecast charts | Shibaco |

What nearly every summary has: the target number in the title or the result bar (RMB 30.4 bn, 4X EBIT, 2.5x revenue), the strategy name, the lettered pillars, and an arrow or triangle from situation to strategy to result.

## 4b. Several figures on one slide (measured on the 163 main-body content slides)

Classified by eye from the pages (covers, agendas, dividers, briefs and appendix excluded). **90% carry two or more exhibits** (78% in 2016–17, 83% in 2019–20).

| Layout | Share | Typical use here |
|---|---|---|
| HALF2 | 33% | analysis + implication; chart left, table or cards right |
| COL3 | 18% | three pillars, three channels, three drivers |
| T1B2 | 11% | executive summaries; one wide chart over two panels |
| R2L1 (one tall left, two right) | 11% | map or bubble chart left, two explanation panels right |
| TABLE | 6% | pillar × row-label matrices (overview, current, strategy, outcome) |
| L2R1 | 5% | two evidence panels left, one conclusion panel right |
| FULL | 4% | frameworks, cycles, customer journeys |
| ROWS, T2B1, COL4, L1R3, GRID4, BAND2 | ≤3% each | |

**New layout seen: L1R3**, a narrow dark side card on the left (photo, brand mark, 3–4 spec lines) with three numbered sections to its right, used on three consecutive slides for three brand lines (YESS 8–10). The side card repeats with the same shape and position, so the three slides read as a set.

`scripts/layout_scan.py` was run as a guide and found a single panel on 93 of 163 content slides: these screenshot decks join their panels with frames, connector arrows and dashed boxes that cross the gutters. The classification above is by eye.

## 6. What not to copy

- Team wordmarks on every slide and organiser logos on the cover (all decks); client logos inside charts. Never copied into house objects.
- P&L and province tables pasted as whole appendix pages at small sizes (TrueNorth 25–31, CVCJ 15–21, YESS 21–23).
- Repeated slides (three decks re-show a slide at the end).
- Photo dividers whose title sits on a busy photo without a panel (Starlight 6, 14, 17 use a solid panel and read well; the others are harder).
- Dense screenshot captures: put the source deck in, not a screen recording, when there is a choice.

## 7. Charts: editable or picture, and how they are rebuilt

- **No source had a chart object.** All 252 pages were pictures (PDF page images, screenshots in .pptx, video frames).
- They were rebuilt into editable slides with `Generators/rebuild_slides.py` (OCR text in English and Chinese + traced shapes), then every OCR line with a number, every line that looked misread, every number and every word the OCR may have missed was checked by eye against the page and corrected (decisions in `review/<key>/ocr-corrections.json`). Charts are now traced shapes with typed labels.
- To change numbers, redraw with the builders rather than editing traced bars; the route for each chart is in `Style/refs/case-competitions-chart-rebuild-map.csv` (rows added for these decks).

## 8. Rules proposed (freeze: filed in `logs/proposals.md`, not applied)

1. **Follow the brief's questions.** When the user gives a brief or a list of questions, give each question its own slide or section, in the brief's order unless the story needs another, and match the executive summary's situation boxes to the brief's topics. (The 13 decks against their 3 briefs.)
2. **Equation slide.** When several facts add up to one conclusion, join the evidence panels with + and = operators to a conclusion panel (a `composeSlide` operator layout). (IIVA 5, CVCJ 5, BASIL 7 and 9.)
3. **Driver formula row.** Show how a target is built as a horizontal formula of boxed drivers with the numbers under each (+ and ×), as an alternative to the vertical value tree. (TrueNorth 21–24, IIVA 10, BeAnInnovator 5.)
4. **Implementation slide.** Close the body with one slide that carries the gantt by pillar, the scenario forecast and the numbered risk map together. (7 of 13 decks.)
5. **Strategy tab bar / acronym banner** as the visible pillar marker on every pillar slide, alongside the breadcrumb. (WhyConsulting, CVCJ, BSTORM.)
6. **L1R3 layout** (side card + three numbered sections) for options shown one per slide in a set. (YESS 8–10.)
7. **Social value next to profit** when the brief or client asks for it: one figure that weighs the two (balance, shared-value panel). (2024 decks.)

## 9. Objects, rebuild quality and reuse (v2.38)

- **OCR review (audit check 12), every deck:** 5,854 lines with a number and 851 lines that looked misread were checked by eye against the page crop, plus 296 numbers and 1,358 word groups the OCR may have missed (white text on red bars, grey on grey, small captions). 1,393 lines were corrected or removed (icons, logos, pins and dashed rules read as letters stay graphics), 218 numbers and 1,265 words were added as text, and the decks were rebuilt with the corrections. The corrected rebuilds were then compared line by line with the reviewed text, and 147 lines that the second pass read differently were fixed in further rounds. Every final deck was rendered and looked at page by page beside its source. Decisions per deck in `review/<key>/` (`ocr-corrections.json` for round 1, `ocr-corrections-round<N>.json` with the `rebuild-info-round<N-1>.json` their ids refer to, `ocr-corrections-final.json` applied to the finished deck, and `ocr-review-notes.md` with the manual fixes and what is still not perfect).
- **Similarity to the pages** (SSIM, 1.0 = identical): 0.59–0.71 for the 13 decks; 0.33–0.53 for the 3 one-page briefs (mostly white pages, where the score says little). Judge by eye.
- **Kept as page pictures:** 7 appendix pages whose tables are too small to rebuild cleanly (Starlight 19–20, WhyConsulting 19–20, YESS 18, 21, 23). Their numbers stay readable on the picture; they are not editable.
- **Still not perfect** (listed per deck in `ocr-review-notes.md`): white text on red or photo bars is sometimes doubled (typed text over traced letter remains); italic is not reproduced (YESS cover and agenda); photos behind agendas are traced into flat colour patches (TrueNorth 3 and 7, Shibaco 15); large-initial acronym banners trace poorly (CVCJ 6); titles that the OCR merged with the breadcrumb were retyped from the slide map (Fusion 2–6 and 8–11).
- **Store:** 252 whole-slide objects, 216 single diagrams and 46 vectorised drawings kept out of 674 candidates, every one looked at on contact sheets. Dropped: logos and wordmarks, product and team photos, photo strips traced into blobs, empty frames, lone text, and drawings that came out broken.
- **Reuse test** (`maintenance/new-reference.md` step 5), rendered and looked at: the TrueNorth store-driver tree (`case21-truenorth-022p2`) places and resizes cleanly; the ChillChili EBIT bridge (`case21-chillchili-007p1`) recolours to another accent, but as a traced chart it keeps fixed label positions, so redraw it with `waterfall` when the numbers change; `extend()` refused the WXYZ risk list (`case22-wxyz-012p2`, not a regular layout) and ran on the BASIL risk table (`case24-basil-013p2`) but the result overlaps (rebuilt table rows are separate text boxes, not row groups), so rebuilt tables should be extended by hand or redrawn with a builder.
