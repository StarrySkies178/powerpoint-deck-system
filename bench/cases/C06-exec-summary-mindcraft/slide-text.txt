[0.4,1.3] Fine jewelry market expects attractive growth in next five years, mainly driven by the strong demands from millennials in tier 1 and 2 cities and emerging middle class from tier 3 and 4 cities. These two primary customer groups have different key purchasing criteria: individuality and youthful image for high-tier millennials and brand recognition for low-tier middle class. / However, CSS faces challenges in seizing growth in the two primary customer groups: low penetration of the youth brands in high tiers and limited access and brand awareness in low tiers. Therefore, CSS’s five-year growth strategy should address branding, offline expansion and omni-channel development with differentiations accommodating to two primary customers. / Moreover, the proposed top-line growth strategy is expected to deliver 12% annual growth, largely driven by offline expansion strategy. / Finally, the strategy will impose affordable financial pressure and the implementation risks including internal resistance, talent and supply chain management and IT system have been preliminarily identified.
