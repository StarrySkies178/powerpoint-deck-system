Layout: tracker, title band, two chart panels side by side, a three-box constraint row, footer

- Top strip: a breadcrumb tracker of three steps joined by arrows. The first step is highlighted and the other two are muted. A company logo sits at the far right.
- Header band: a two-line action title.
- Upper body: two panels side by side. Each opens with a numbered circle, a heading and an underline rule.
  - Left panel: a chart title with a unit line under it, then a stacked column chart. It has three columns (years, the last one an estimate) and two stacked series, with a total label above each column and axis-break marks between the columns. To its right is a small growth-rate table with two period columns and three rows (two segments and a total). Row labels sit on its left. One cell is circled with a hand-drawn ellipse.
  - Right panel: a vertical divider splits it into two small charts, each with a title and a unit line. The first is a two-column chart (two years, one series), with value labels above the columns and an arrow over them carrying a growth rate in an oval. The second is a single 100% stacked column of two segments, with segment names on its left and percentage labels on its right on leader lines. A break mark cuts across the column.
- Lower body: a third numbered heading with an underline rule, then a filled box split by vertical dividers into three columns. Each column has an icon beside a bold heading, with an italic description below.
- Footer: a note line, a rule, then the sources line on the left and the page number on the right.
