# Layout: header title, three stacked scenario tables, footer

- **Header band:** a single-line action title at top left, with a horizontal rule running across the full content width directly beneath it. No breadcrumb, tracker or subtitle.
- **Body:** one figure, a set of three separate tables stacked top to bottom with a blank gap between each. No chart, no side panel, no callouts. The body ends with empty space below the last table; there is no takeaway bar.
- **Each table (all three identical in structure):**
  - Six columns: a label column on the left, then five numeric year columns spread evenly across the rest of the width. Numbers are right-aligned in their columns; labels are left-aligned.
  - One header row: the scenario name in bold in the label column, and the five year headings in bold.
  - Three data rows: first a count row (whole numbers), then two percentage rows.
  - Horizontal rules only: one under the header row and one under the first data row. No rule between the two percentage rows or under the last row. No vertical lines, no fills or shading.
  - The three tables share the same column alignment, so the year columns line up vertically across all three.
- **Footer band:** a thin horizontal rule across the width, then below it a source note at the left, and at the right two small logos side by side separated by a short vertical divider (client logo first, firm logo second), followed by the page number at the far right.
