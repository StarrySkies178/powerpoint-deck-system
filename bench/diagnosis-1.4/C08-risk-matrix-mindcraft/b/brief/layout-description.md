# Layout: risk heat-map matrix beside a four-row risk/solution table

- **Header:** a tracker of three section names separated by arrow markers, with the last one highlighted. Below it is a two-line action title, then a full-width rule.
- **Body:** two parts side by side. Each opens with a numbered section label (number in a small square, bold heading, underline rule).
- **Left, risk matrix:** a 5 x 5 heat-map grid, shaded darker toward the top-right. Each axis has an arrow, a title and three level labels (low, medium, high). Eight numbered circle markers sit in cells. Markers 1 to 4 sit unlabelled inside a dashed rectangle around the upper-right block, with a short label in its top-left corner. Markers 5 to 8 sit outside it, each with a short label beside it. There are three marker styles: 1 and 2, 3 and 4, and 5 to 8.
- **Connector:** one right-pointing chevron between the matrix and the table.
- **Right, table:** two column headers over a rule, then four rows, with the second and fourth shaded. Each row has a numbered circle outside it on the left, in the same style as that risk's marker in the matrix. The left cell holds a bold heading and one bullet. The right cell holds two short paragraphs, each opening with a bold bracketed tag (avoid, then mitigate).
- **Footer:** a thin rule, with a source note on the left and the logo plus page number on the right.
