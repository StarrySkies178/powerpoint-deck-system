# Layout: four-column strategy table under a tracker and a title band

- **Top strip:** a breadcrumb tracker of three steps joined by arrow markers; the middle (current) step stands out, the other two are muted. A firm logo sits at its far right.
- **Title band:** a full-width filled band below the tracker holding a two-line headline.
- **Body: one table with four columns**, two wider columns on the left and two narrower ones on the right, separated by gaps.
  - Row 1, column headers: a shaded header cell over each column; the first three carry a label, the fourth is empty.
  - Row 2, summary row: a strongly filled cell per column. Columns 1 and 2 hold short bulleted lists (three and two bullets); columns 3 and 4 hold short unbulleted lines (two and three lines).
  - Rows 3 to 6, detail rows: columns 1 and 2 each hold four stacked lightly shaded boxes, paired side by side across the two columns (a phase beside its matching strategy). Each box has a bold heading and three or four bullets (three, three, four, three in each column).
  - Column 3 is one tall shaded box spanning all four detail rows, holding two sub-headed groups of three bullets each.
  - Column 4 is likewise one tall box, holding one list of four bullets, with a gap before the last bullet.
- **Footer:** a full-width shaded band holding a source line on the left; no page number.
