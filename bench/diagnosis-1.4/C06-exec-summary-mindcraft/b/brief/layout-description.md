# Layout: text-only executive summary on a full-bleed shaded background

- One full-bleed background covers the whole slide. It fades steadily from the left edge to the right edge, with no bands, boxes or dividing lines.
- There is no separate header band. The slide title sits directly on the background at the top left, as a single short line.
- Below the title is one text block of four prose paragraphs, stacked top to bottom with a small gap between paragraphs. There are no bullets, numbers, sub-headings or icons.
- The paragraphs differ in length: the first and second are the longest (about five lines each), the third is the shortest (two lines) and the fourth is mid-length (three lines). They read as a sequence: market context, challenges and strategy, the expected growth result, then financial pressure and risks.
- The title and the text block share the same left edge. The text block covers only the left part of the slide, and the right side is left empty, with only the background showing.
- All body text is in one style. Nothing is highlighted or called out inside the paragraphs.
- There is no chart, table, figure, breadcrumb or tracker, takeaway bar, logo, source line, footnote or page number anywhere on the slide.
