Executive summary framework: three stage columns by two rows, funnelling into one takeaway bar

- Header band: a two-line action title, with a horizontal rule beneath it.
- Stage tracker: a row of three outlined arrow tabs (flat left end, pointed right end), each with a short bold stage name, sitting above three content columns. Nothing sits above the row-label column on the left.
- Row labels: a left column of two filled label boxes, one per row, each with a short bold label. The first box is much taller than the second.
- First row (a three-column grid): each column holds a bulleted list of three bold headings, separated by dotted vertical dividers.
  - First column: the first heading has two dash sub-items; the other two have none.
  - Second column: the first two headings each have two dash sub-items; the third has none (in the original it runs into the row below).
  - Third column: three headings, no sub-items.
- Between the rows: two horizontal lines (one under the first column, one under the second and third), each with a downward double-chevron marker centred on it.
- Second row: two cells split by a dotted divider. The left cell (under the first column) holds one bulleted sentence. The right cell spans the second and third columns and holds one bulleted sentence with a bold quoted phrase.
- Convergence: one horizontal line across the full content width with a single downward chevron marker in the middle.
- Takeaway bar: one filled, rounded bar across the content width with a single bold centred sentence.
- Footer: a thin rule, a source note on the left, two client/firm logos separated by a thin divider and a page number on the right.
